Misbahati - Tasbih Counter

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Privacy Policy

Misbahati does not collect any data about you.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and does not send any information to any server.
  • It contains no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics.
  • It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

What is stored on your device?

The app stores the following on your device only: your counter number and the list of adhkar (remembrances) you create yourself, including their names, daily targets, and your daily progress, plus your app settings (such as vibration, sound, and counting method). None of this ever leaves your device. Deleting the app removes all of it, and you can edit or delete your adhkar, or reset the counter, at any time inside the app.

Apple analytics

Apple may provide me with aggregated, anonymous statistics through App Store Connect, such as download counts, only for users who choose to share such data in their device settings. These statistics do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app works ever changes, I will update this page and the date above.
